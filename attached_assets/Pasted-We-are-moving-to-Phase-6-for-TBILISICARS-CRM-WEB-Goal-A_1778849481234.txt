We are moving to Phase 6 for TBILISICARS CRM&WEB.

Goal:
Audit and plan targeted mobile responsiveness fixes for both the public Website and CRM. The issue is that on iOS/Android some pages stretch horizontally, move sideways, overflow, or modals/tables do not fit the screen cleanly.

Important:
- PLAN ONLY. Do not implement yet.
- Do not redesign the website or CRM.
- Do not change business logic.
- Do not change API routes.
- Do not change database schema.
- Do not change pricing, availability, booking creation, payment logic, email/PDF, or CRM workflow logic.
- Do not do broad refactors.
- This must be a targeted mobile overflow/responsiveness stabilization plan.

Primary goal:
Find and fix sources of unwanted horizontal overflow and mobile layout instability.

Highest-priority CRM issue:
Booking Detail mobile/modal stability.

When a booking is opened in CRM, the Booking Detail window/modal/page must remain visually stable on iOS and Android after any action:
- uploading pickup/dropoff photos
- completing Pick Up
- completing Drop Off
- adding payment
- adding/changing assigned vehicle
- unassigning vehicle
- adding notes/activity/history
- expanding/collapsing sections
- showing original booked car line
- showing delivery address rows
- showing payment records or photo lists

Required Booking Detail behavior:
- The booking window must not grow wider than the mobile viewport.
- No horizontal page scroll should appear.
- The modal/page must not move sideways.
- Long text, vehicle names, license plates, phone/email, payment rows, activity rows, image filenames, and delivery addresses must wrap or truncate safely.
- Photo upload previews/lists must stay inside the container.
- Tables or rows inside Booking Detail must stack or scroll internally if needed, without stretching the whole page.
- Bottom/action buttons must remain visible and tappable.
- Layout must remain stable before and after Add Payment, Pick Up, Drop Off, and photo upload.
- Fix root causes, not only by hiding overflow globally.

Technical audit requirements:
Search for likely mobile overflow causes:
- fixed widths
- min-width values
- w-screen / 100vw usage
- tables wider than viewport
- grid columns that do not collapse on small screens
- modals with fixed width or max-width that still overflow
- inputs/buttons in one row that should stack on mobile
- long text without wrapping
- overflow-x behavior
- absolute/fixed elements that extend outside viewport
- popovers/dropdowns not constrained to viewport
- images/cards without max-width
- uploaded image previews or filenames that force width
- payment/history/activity rows that force width
- any recent Phase 1–5 additions that may cause overflow on mobile

Plan requirements:
1. List exact files/components where mobile overflow is likely.
2. Specifically analyze BookingDetail.tsx and identify why it can still grow/move after payment/photo/pickup/dropoff actions.
3. Separate fixes into:
   A) Booking Detail stability fixes
   B) global safe CSS/container fixes
   C) website component-specific fixes
   D) other CRM component-specific fixes
4. For every proposed change, explain why it is safe and what it affects.
5. Avoid hiding real content with overflow-x:hidden unless used only as a safety guard and not as a substitute for fixing the actual layout.
6. Identify which fixes are low-risk and can be implemented first.
7. Identify any high-risk areas that should be left for manual testing or later.
8. List exact files proposed to change.
