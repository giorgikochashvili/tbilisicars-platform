Desired payment behavior:
- Every manually added payment means real money was received.
- Booking Payment must count toward Total Paid.
- Additional Payment must count toward Total Paid.
- Extra Days Payment must count toward Total Paid.
- All three should create accounting income entries.
- All three should affect booking payment status calculation because they are real collected money.

Business rule for extra days / extensions:
- If a customer extends the rental during an active booking/pickup/delivered state, staff may edit the dropoff date/time.
- Changing date/time must NOT automatically recalculate price.
- We eventually want the system to ask staff for an optional extra charge amount when date/time is changed.
- Example:
  - Booking total: EUR 300
  - Paid: EUR 300
  - Remaining: EUR 0
  - Staff extends dropoff by one day and enters extra charge EUR 30
  - Booking total becomes EUR 330
  - Paid remains EUR 300
  - Remaining becomes EUR 30
  - No payment row is created yet.
  - No accounting income row is created yet.
  - Later, when the customer pays EUR 30, staff adds Payment Type = Extra Days Payment, amount EUR 30.
  - Then Total Paid becomes EUR 330 and Remaining becomes EUR 0.
- This extension/extra-charge flow may be a separate Phase 2B. For now, inspect feasibility only. Do not implement it yet unless explicitly approved later.
