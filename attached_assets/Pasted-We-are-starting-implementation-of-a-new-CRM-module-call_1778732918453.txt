We are starting implementation of a new CRM module called “Digital Showroom” for TBILISICARS.

IMPORTANT:
This is Phase 1 only. Keep the scope small, isolated, additive, and rollback-safe.

The previous analysis plan is approved only partially. Apply the corrections below:
- Do NOT add canManageShowroom or any new permission column in v1.
- Digital Showroom should be visible to all CRM users for now.
- Do NOT add video upload in v1.
- Do NOT add device assignment in v1.
- Do NOT connect Showroom prices to existing Rates, Pricing, Promotions, Booking quote, or public booking logic.
- Do NOT modify existing vehicle image upload logic.
- Use existing vehicle model images read-only.
- Do NOT show technical specs such as transmission, seats, doors, fuel type, or engine details.
- Text and badges must be visual overlays only, not edits to the original PNG/image files.

Project context:
TBILISICARS CRM&WEB is a live production system. Digital Showroom must be added as a new independent CRM module for airport/office staff to visually present vehicle models to walk-in customers on a tablet or screen.

Digital Showroom v1 purpose:
- Category-first vehicle presentation for fast desk sales.
- Use existing transparent vehicle model PNG/images.
- Allow manual sales/promo text overlays.
- Support English, Hebrew, Arabic text fields.
- Support separate manual Showroom Prices in USD, with EUR calculated from a manual showroom exchange rate.
- Prepare foundation for slides, playlists, compare mode, and fullscreen slideshow, but do not implement everything in one step.
