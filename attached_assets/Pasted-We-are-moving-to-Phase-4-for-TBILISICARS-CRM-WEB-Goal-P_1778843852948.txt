Current business problem:
CRM staff manually creates bookings, but:
- Vehicle section currently comes before pickup/dropoff location and date fields.
- Price does not auto-fill from website rates.
- Currency defaults to GEL, but it should default to EUR.
- One-way fee is not clearly auto-added.
- Optional extras are not selectable with quantities in the manual booking form.
- Staff should still be able to manually edit the amount after auto-fill.

Required form order:
1) Customer
2) Reservation
3) Pickup / Return details
   - pickup location
   - pickup date & time
   - dropoff/return location
   - return date & time
4) Vehicle
   - brand
   - model
   - specific vehicle optional
5) Optional Extras
   - active extras list
   - checkbox/select
   - quantity
6) Pricing & Notes
   - pricing summary
   - amount field
   - currency default EUR
   - payment status
   - notes

Required price behavior:
- Once pickup/dropoff dates, pickup/dropoff locations, and vehicle model are selected, CRM should calculate the same price as the website would show for that model/duration/location.
- Example: Mitsubishi Attrage, 7 days, website rate = 180 EUR → amount field auto-fills 180 EUR.
- If pickup/dropoff requires one-way fee, include it automatically and show it in pricing summary.
- Optional extras should be included in total when selected, with quantity.
- Amount field must remain editable. Staff can directly change the amount without complicated UI.
- Do not overwrite staff-entered amount in a confusing way. Plan how to avoid accidental overwrite, but keep UX simple.
