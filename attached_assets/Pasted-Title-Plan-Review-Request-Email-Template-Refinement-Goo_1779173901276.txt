Title: Plan Review Request Email Template Refinement — Google-focused branded email + Trustpilot separate invite note

Use PLAN MODE only. Do not edit files yet.

Context:
The Review Request feature is already live and technically works.
We tested Trustpilot + Google review request:
- Tbilisicars branded email is sent from our system via Resend
- Trustpilot official invitation email is sent separately via Trustpilot AFS/BCC

We now want to refine the customer-facing Tbilisicars email.

Final product decision:
The Tbilisicars branded email should be primarily a Google review request email.
If Trustpilot is selected too, the Tbilisicars email should politely explain that a separate official Trustpilot review invitation may also be received.

Important:
Do not change the core Review Request flow yet.
Do not change returned-only eligibility.
Do not change Trustpilot BCC security.
Do not expose TRUSTPILOT_BCC_EMAIL.
Do not add QR code in this first phase unless you only recommend it for later.

Approved email copy:
Use this exact approved text in the Tbilisicars branded email:

“If you have a moment, we would be grateful if you could share your experience with Tbilisicars on Google. Your review helps other travelers choose us with confidence.

You may also receive a separate official review invitation from Trustpilot. Thank you for taking the time to support us.”

Important behavior for this text:
- The first paragraph should appear when a Google destination is selected.
- The Trustpilot sentence should appear only when Trustpilot is selected.
- If Trustpilot is not selected, do not mention Trustpilot.
- If Google is not selected, inspect and explain what should happen before Build.

Required changes to plan:
1. Make the Tbilisicars email Google-focused.
2. Remove Trustpilot button/link from the Tbilisicars branded email if Trustpilot official invitation is already being sent separately.
3. Add or improve Google button:
   - should look clearly like a Google action
   - include Google logo or safe Google-style mark
   - label:
     “Review us on Google – Tbilisi”
     or
     “Review us on Google – Kutaisi”
4. Add Tbilisicars logo to the email header.
   - Do not place the logo inline before “Thank you” inside a red header.
   - The current red-heavy header may clash with the logo.
   - Prefer a clean, premium, centered logo/header layout.
   - Use red only as a small accent if needed.
5. Remove reservation / booking reference from the email entirely.
   Reason:
   Customers may book through brokers and their broker reference may not match our internal TC reference.
   Do not show “TC-000828”.
   Do not show “#828”.
   Do not show any booking reference in this review email.
6. Replace:
   “The Tbilisicars Team”
   with:
   “Tbilisicars Team”
7. Keep wording short, friendly, professional, and non-incentivized.
8. Do not offer gifts, discounts, rewards, or any incentive.
9. Do not add fake review counts.
10. Do not add aggregateRating schema.

QR code:
We like the idea of a Google review QR code, but treat it as Phase 2.
In this plan, inspect and report only:
- Is there already a QR code dependency?
- What would be the safest future QR implementation?
- Resend inline/CID image vs hosted static image vs other option
- Email client compatibility risks
- Do not implement QR in this phase unless you explicitly recommend it and explain why it is zero-risk.
