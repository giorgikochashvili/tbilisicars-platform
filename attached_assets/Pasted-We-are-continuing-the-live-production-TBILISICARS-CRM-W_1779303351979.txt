Now plan Phase 4:
Advance Payment / Pending Receivables.

Important terminology — do not confuse these concepts:

1. Normal Booking Payment / Additional Payment / Extra Days Payment
These are real received payments.
They create booking_payment rows.
They create accounting income entries.
They count toward Total Paid.
They affect paymentStatus.

2. Advance Payment / Pending Receivable
This is NOT real received income yet.
This is money expected from a broker/partner/platform/company later.
The customer may already have paid the broker/partner, but Tbilisicars has not received the money yet.
Pending advance amount must NOT count as real income.
Pending advance amount must NOT create accounting_entries yet.
Pending advance amount must NOT count toward Total Paid until received.
Pending advance amount must be separately visible as Pending Receivable.

3. Mark as Received
This is the moment Tbilisicars actually receives the partner/broker payout.
Only then should the system:
- mark the advance payment/receivable as received
- create accounting income entry
- count it toward Total Paid
- update paymentStatus
- remove it from pending receivables

Correct example:

Initial booking:
- booking.totalAmount = EUR 300
- totalPaid = EUR 0
- remainingBalance = EUR 300
- paymentStatus = UNPAID

Customer paid broker/partner EUR 300 before pickup.
Tbilisicars has not received the payout yet.

Staff records an Advance Payment / Pending Receivable:
- expected amount = EUR 300
- method/source = Advance Payment / Broker payout
- status = PENDING

After recording pending receivable:
- booking.totalAmount = EUR 300
- totalPaid remains EUR 0
- remainingBalance remains EUR 300
- paymentStatus remains UNPAID, unless current business wants PREPAID display separately
- NO accounting_entries row is created
- It appears in Pending Receivables list/report

Later partner/broker transfers EUR 300 to Tbilisicars.

Staff clicks Mark as Received:
- pending receivable status becomes RECEIVED
- accounting income entry is created
- totalPaid becomes EUR 300
- remainingBalance becomes EUR 0
- paymentStatus becomes PAID

Business goal:
We need a safe way to track expected money from brokers/partners/platforms separately from real income, and then convert it to received income only when staff confirms the money arrived.
