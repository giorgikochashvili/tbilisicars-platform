# TASK — Fix Step 1 / Step 2 Pricing Mismatch (Child vs Parent Rate)

We need to fix a confirmed pricing inconsistency in the public booking flow.

---

## 🔴 PROBLEM

Step 1 (vehicle selection) shows the correct seasonal/child rate,  
but Step 2+ (booking summary and all later steps) sometimes switch to a higher parent/base rate.

---

## 📊 CONFIRMED EXAMPLE

Search:
- Pickup: Tbilisi International Airport
- Dropoff: Kutaisi International Airport
- Dates: 05 May 2026 09:00 → 12 May 2026 09:00
- Duration: 7 days
- Vehicle: Mitsubishi Attrage
- One-way fee: 50 EUR
- Discount: 15%

Step 1 (correct):
- 25 EUR/day × 7 = 175
- Discount = -26.25
- Rental = 148.75
- +50 one-way fee
- TOTAL = 198.75 EUR

Step 2 (WRONG):
- 35 EUR/day × 7 = 245
- Discount = -36.75
- Rental = 208.25
- +50 one-way fee
- TOTAL = 258.25 EUR

---

## 🧠 ROOT CAUSE (CONFIRMED)

In:
artifacts/api-server/src/routes/public-bookings.ts

The Step 1 endpoint:
GET /api/public/booking-config

uses SQL that resolves rates using:

CURRENT_DATE

But Step 2+:
POST /api/public/quote

uses resolveRateTier() with:
pickupDate (correct)

So Step 1 and Step 2 use DIFFERENT date references.

---

## 🎯 GOAL

Make Step 1 use the SAME rate resolution logic as Step 2:
👉 based on the USER’S PICKUP DATE

NOT today's date.

---

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT RULES

- DO NOT modify frontend
- DO NOT touch Booking.tsx
- DO NOT touch CRM
- DO NOT modify discount logic
- DO NOT modify one-way fee logic
- DO NOT rewrite pricing system
- DO NOT change resolveRateTier()

ONLY fix booking-config SQL date logic

---

## 🛠️ IMPLEMENTATION

### FILE:
artifacts/api-server/src/routes/public-bookings.ts

---

### STEP 1 — Add rateCheckDate

Before the priceLateral SQL block, define:

const rateCheckDate = filterByDates
  ? `'${new Date(pickupDt!).toISOString().slice(0, 10)}'`
  : "CURRENT_DATE";

---

### STEP 2 — Replace ALL CURRENT_DATE usages

Inside the priceLateral SQL block, replace ALL occurrences of:

CURRENT_DATE

with:

${rateCheckDate}

Specifically these 4 lines:

r.valid_from::date <= CURRENT_DATE
r.valid_until::date >= CURRENT_DATE
r2.valid_from::date <= CURRENT_DATE
r2.valid_until::date >= CURRENT_DATE

---

## 🧠 RESULT

Now Step 1 will resolve rates using:
pickup date (e.g. 2026-05-05)

Same as Step 2 quote endpoint.
