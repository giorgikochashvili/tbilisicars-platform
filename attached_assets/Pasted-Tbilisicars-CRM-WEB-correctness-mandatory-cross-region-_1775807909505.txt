Tbilisicars CRM&WEB — correctness + mandatory cross-region fleet visibility fix (surgical, no refactor)

We are fixing a critical lifecycle correctness issue AND preserving required operational visibility for destination regions.

Business rule:
A vehicle that is currently rented from one region and will definitely return to another region must remain truthfully located in its current region until RETURNED, BUT it must also be visible in the destination region’s fleet/vehicle list as an inbound vehicle before return.

Example:
Vehicle goes out in Tbilisi and will return in Kutaisi.
Before RETURNED:

* current location truth must remain Tbilisi
* Kutaisi team must still see that vehicle in Kutaisi vehicle list/fleet context
* Kutaisi team must understand it is inbound / expected return, not physically already there
* assignment workflows must continue to work

## PART 2 — Mandatory destination-region visibility

File:
artifacts/api-server/src/services/admin-fleet.service.ts

Goal:
When filtering vehicles by city, the result must include:

1. vehicles physically in that city now
2. vehicles inbound to that city from another region

Implementation requirement:

When filters.city is active:

1. Keep existing vehicle query based on vehicle.locationId

2. Add inbound vehicle query for vehicles that:

* are RENTED
* are not already in the result set
* have an active DELIVERED booking
* that booking’s dropoffLocationId belongs to the requested city

3. Merge them into the same city result list

4. Mark them with:
   inboundToRegion: true

Important:
These inbound vehicles MUST appear in the destination city vehicle list/fleet result.
This is required behavior, not optional.

Do NOT fake current location.
Do NOT modify vehicle.locationId for this.
