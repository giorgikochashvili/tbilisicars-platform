Now plan Phase 5B:
Fixed Monthly Expenses CRM UI.

Business goal:
Finance/staff should be able to manage fixed monthly expense templates from the Accounting module UI and manually post them for a selected month.

Important business rules:
1. Fixed Expense Template itself is NOT an accounting expense.
2. Expense is created only when staff clicks “Post for month”.
3. Posting creates one accounting_entries EXPENSE row.
4. No automatic cron/monthly posting.
5. Duplicate post for same template/month must be clearly blocked.
6. Deactivating template must not remove posted accounting entries.
7. Existing Accounting ledger and manual entry CRUD must remain unchanged.
8. No booking/payment logic should change.
9. No public website changes.
