==================================================

There must be one real booking system.

Website bookings and CRM bookings must NOT live in disconnected parallel systems.

The website must feed the CRM booking pipeline using the same or safely compatible booking structures.

Do NOT create:

B. REQUIRED OUTCOME
==================================================

When a customer submits a booking request on the website:

1. the booking is validated
2. the selected vehicle/model/rate data is processed safely
3. customer data is captured
4. extras/promotions are applied if already supported
5. a real booking is created in CRM-compatible format
6. the booking becomes visible in the CRM Bookings module
7. the booking affects Fleet Calendar / Timeline, Alerts, Dashboard, and reporting where applicable
