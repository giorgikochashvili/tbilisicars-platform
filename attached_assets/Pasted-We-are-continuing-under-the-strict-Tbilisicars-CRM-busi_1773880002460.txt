- date-based warnings
- clear severity levels

These alerts must integrate with:

- Vehicle Detail
- Alerts module
- Dashboard alert panel

==================================================
A. ALERT TYPES
==================================================

Extend the service alert logic to support these states:

1. SERVICE_WARNING
Service approaching soon.

Conditions:
- next_service_date within 7 days
OR
- mileage within 1000 km of service threshold

2. SERVICE_DUE
Service must be performed now.

Conditions:
- next_service_date = today
OR
- mileage >= service threshold

3. SERVICE_OVERDUE
Service is late.

Conditions:
- next_service_date < today
OR
- mileage exceeded threshold by >1000 km
