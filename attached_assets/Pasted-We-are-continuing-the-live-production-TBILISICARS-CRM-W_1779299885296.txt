Now plan Phase 3.

Business goal:
Add booking-level manual Deposit Amount + separate Deposit Currency to Booking Detail, and simplify Payment Summary.

Important business rules:
- Deposit is metadata only.
- Deposit is NOT a payment.
- Deposit must NOT create booking_payment rows.
- Deposit must NOT create accounting_entries rows.
- Deposit must NOT increase Total Paid.
- Deposit must NOT reduce Remaining Balance.
- Deposit must NOT affect paymentStatus.
- Deposit must NOT be treated as income or expense.
- Deposit should simply store and display the agreed/recorded deposit amount and deposit currency for the booking.

Desired UI behavior:
Booking Detail should allow staff to enter/edit:
- Deposit Amount
- Deposit Currency

Deposit Currency must be separate from booking currency.
Allowed currencies:
- GEL
- USD
- EUR

Payment Summary cleanup:
Remove/hide from the CRM Payment Summary card:
- Deposit Returned
- Total Refunded
- Net Deposit

Show clean summary:
- Total Paid
- Remaining Balance
- Deposit Amount + Deposit Currency

If Deposit Amount is empty/0, display something clean such as “—” or “Not set”, depending on current UI style.
