Now plan Phase 2B:
Extension Charge / Additional Amount Due flow for active booking extensions.

Important terminology — do not confuse these two concepts:

1. Extension Charge / Additional Amount Due
This is NOT a payment.
This is an additional amount added to the booking total when staff extends the rental/dropoff date and decides the customer owes more money.
It increases booking.totalAmount and therefore increases Remaining Balance.
It must NOT create a booking_payment row.
It must NOT create an accounting_entries row.
It must NOT count as paid.

2. Extra Days Payment
This IS a payment.
This is used later only when the customer actually pays the extra amount.
Staff records it from Booking Detail → Payment History → New Payment Entry with Payment Type = EXTRA_DAYS_PAYMENT.
Only then Total Paid increases and accounting income is created.

Correct example:

Initial state:
- booking.totalAmount = EUR 300
- totalPaid = EUR 300
- remainingBalance = EUR 0

Customer extends the rental by 1 day.
Staff edits dropoff date/time.
Staff enters Extension Charge / Additional Amount Due = EUR 30.

After saving extension charge:
- booking.totalAmount = EUR 330
- totalPaid remains EUR 300
- remainingBalance becomes EUR 30
- NO booking_payment row is created
- NO accounting_entries row is created

Later, when customer pays EUR 30:
Staff adds payment:
- Payment Type = EXTRA_DAYS_PAYMENT
- Amount = EUR 30

After payment:
- booking.totalAmount = EUR 330
- totalPaid = EUR 330
- remainingBalance = EUR 0
- accounting income entry is created

Business problem:
When a customer extends an active rental, staff may edit the booking dropoff date/time. The date/time change must NOT automatically recalculate price. But staff should be able to optionally enter an Extension Charge / Additional Amount Due for the extension. This charge should increase booking.totalAmount and therefore increase Remaining Balance, without creating a payment row yet.

Important business rules:
1. Changing dropoff date/time must not automatically calculate price.
2. Extension Charge / Additional Amount Due must be manually entered by staff.
3. Extension Charge is optional.
4. Extension Charge increases booking.totalAmount only.
5. Extension Charge does not create booking_payment.
6. Extension Charge does not create accounting_entries.
7. Extension Charge does not count as paid.
8. Extension Charge should be in booking currency unless existing booking edit logic requires otherwise.
9. Existing paid amount must remain unchanged.
10. Existing payment rows must remain unchanged.
11. Existing deposit logic from Phase 3 must remain unchanged.
12. Extra Days Payment remains separate and is used only when money is actually received.
