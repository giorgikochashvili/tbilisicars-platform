Now plan Phase 5:
Fixed Monthly Expenses.

Business goal:
Finance should be able to manage recurring/fixed monthly expenses such as:
- Office rent
- Airport office fee
- Parking rent
- Software subscriptions
- Fixed salary
- Accountant service
- Internet / phone
- Other fixed monthly expenses

Important business rules:
1. Fixed Monthly Expenses are templates/planned recurring expenses.
2. A template itself is NOT an accounting expense.
3. Accounting expense should be created only when staff manually posts the template for a specific month.
4. No automatic cron/monthly posting in Phase 5.
5. Avoid duplicate posting for the same template and month.
6. Posted expense should create a normal accounting_entries EXPENSE row.
7. Existing Accounting reports should include posted fixed expenses because they become normal accounting_entries.
8. Template creation/edit/delete should be audit-logged if possible.
9. No booking_payment rows should be created.
10. No booking/payment logic should change.
